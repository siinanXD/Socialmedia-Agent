# Product brief

## Audience and scope
A multi-tenant creator tool, with PXUL WRLD as first pilot. Support other niches through configuration, not fitness-specific logic.

## User journey
1. Creator registers/configures brand, platforms, tone, target audience and legal permissions.
2. Uploads multiple videos/photos or opts into a watched folder.
3. Async jobs detect scenes, speech, visual action and high-quality moments; output timestamped highlights with provenance, confidence and editable rankings.
4. Creative director proposes hooks, storyboards and original variants, based on optional sourced trend references.
5. Renderer produces 9:16 drafts, subtitles, covers, captions and image carousels; supports download and preview.
6. Creator approves publication. Authorized platform integrations may publish and record canonical post IDs.
7. Insights (when authorized) feed cautious comparisons at 24h, 72h and 7d. Small samples are flagged; no viral guarantees.

## Trend intelligence
Use approved platform APIs, permitted public references or creator-submitted links. Track source URL, collection timestamp, region, niche, hook, pacing, editing pattern, text overlay, music metadata and missing fields. Do not clone another creator's footage or assume trending audio is licensed. License checks are account/region/platform specific.

## MVP success
Given a batch of multiple creator-owned videos/photos, the system outputs at least two reproducible downloadable vertical drafts with source time ranges, subtitle files, cover and caption proposals, a JSON manifest and explicit approval state. All nonfunctional features must be clearly marked planned.
